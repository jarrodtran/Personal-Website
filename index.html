02 / How I operate

Think from first principles.
Operate as a system.

I start by separating facts from assumptions and mapping how the system actually behaves. Then I remove what does not serve the outcome, make the decision and ownership explicit, and build the feedback loop that lets the solution scale.

  1. 01

    Ground truth

    Reduce the mandate to its outcome, constraints, economics, and assumptions.

  2. 02

    System map

    Trace the dependencies, incentives, bottlenecks, and feedback loops.

  3. 03

    Simplify

    Remove work, requirements, and complexity that do not serve the outcome.

  4. 04

    Commit

    Make the tradeoff, accountable owner, and next move explicit.

  5. 05

    Compound

    Install the cadence, feedback, and selective automation that let the system improve as it scales.

That is the environment I try to create: rigorous enough to earn trust, simple enough to use, and calm enough for people to do their best work.
