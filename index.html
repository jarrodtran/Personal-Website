How I work

Get the facts.
Keep the work simple.

I spend the first stretch figuring out what’s actually true: the numbers, the constraints, who owns what. Then I cut the extra, make a call, and put a rhythm in place so the factory doesn’t need me standing over it.

  1. 01

    Get the facts

    Write down the outcome, the constraints, and what’s assumed versus proven.

  2. 02

    Map it

    Follow the work, the money, and the bottlenecks until you can see the whole thing.

  3. 03

    Cut extra

    Drop requirements and meetings that don’t move the number.

  4. 04

    Decide

    Name the tradeoff, the owner, and the next move.

  5. 05

    Keep going

    Set a weekly rhythm. Automate the parts that repeat.
