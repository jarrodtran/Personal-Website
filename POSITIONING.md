The homepage introduces Jarrod, lists the latest essays with a way to subscribe, links four case studies, gives a short bio, and invites conversation. /writing/ is the full essay archive.

## Publishing

The homepage reads the Substack feed when the static site builds. After publishing on Substack, run the GitHub Pages deployment workflow to refresh the site. When the feed is empty, visitors can subscribe. When the feed is unavailable, visitors can open the publication directly.
